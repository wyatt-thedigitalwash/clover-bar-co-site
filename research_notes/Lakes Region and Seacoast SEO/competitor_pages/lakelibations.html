Curated Cocktails. Memorable Moments. Local Love.

Our roots are as local as our ingredients. Our story began in a Meredith NH kitchen, where nights were filled with close friends, experimental cocktails, and endless laughter. When our hobby farm chapter closed, one old horse trailer sparked something new. With a little grit (and a lot of love), Lake Libations was born.

With decades of business experience and a deep love for event planning, we bring energy, reliability, and a personal touch to every gathering. As proud NH locals, we love this community and that passion is the secret ingredient in everything we do!

We proudly offer:

  • Craft Cocktail Menus tailored to your season, style, and preferences

Dry Hire: How it Works

Hiring a mobile bar lets you, the host, relax and enjoy your party while we craft and serve exceptional cocktails using fresh herbs, juices, syrups, and house-made infusions – always delivered with style and flair.

Lake Libations is a Dry-Hire Mobile Bartending Company based in the Lakes Region of New Hampshire. That means you supply the alcohol, and we bring everything else: the professional bar setup, premium glassware, hand-prepared garnishes, and of course, our charismatic bartenders to elevate your celebration!

We’ll work with you to design a personalized cocktail menu tailored to your taste and theme, and we’ll supply an alcohol shopping list so you know exactly what and how much to buy. Best of all, when the party ends, you keep any unused alcohol.

From intimate backyard parties to wedding receptions and corporate events, our mobile bar trailer and sleek pop-up bar bring a unique, interactive experience your guests will remember.

Because honestly – who doesn’t love a bar on wheels?
