# NH Seacoast Local Market Fact File (Mobile BYOB Bartending)

Research date: 2026-10-20. Every fact below has a source link. Some of these sources are directory listings (Zola, Wedding-Spot, WeddingWire) or third-party summaries. They are marked as such and should be checked against the venue before they go into copy. Note: several primary pages could not be fetched directly (the NH State Parks Seacoast Weddings brochure PDF and the Justia rule page timed out or returned 403). Those facts come from search-result excerpts of the primary documents.

## 1. Venues and their alcohol / outside-bartender policies

### Takeaway
The biggest Seacoast venues split into two groups. Some are in-house-only (Wentworth by the Sea bans outside beverages). Others are "raw space" or rental venues where an outside licensed bar service is expected (Seacoast Science Center at Odiorne Point, probably Saltonstall Farm). Almost every venue with a published policy requires a paid police detail when alcohol is served. The Seacoast Science Center also requires the bar service to hold a valid NH liquor license. That matters a lot for a BYOB-only model, and Clover should confirm its own licensing position (see Gaps).

### Cited Findings

**Wentworth by the Sea (oceanfront grand hotel, New Castle/Rye border, overlooking Little Harbor)**
- Policy page: "no outside food or beverage may be brought into the event" (the venue cites state law). Bartender fee of $150 per bartender for cash/consumption bars. The bar closes 30 minutes before the event ends. — [Wentworth guidelines and policies](https://www.opalcollection.com/wentworth/restaurants/wedding-menus/menu/guidelines-and-policies)
- A police detail is required "for all events over 75 people and alcohol is being served," at $75/hour. — [Wentworth guidelines and policies](https://www.opalcollection.com/wentworth/restaurants/wedding-menus/menu/guidelines-and-policies)
- Outdoor ceremonies are held on "The Lawn" near the 17th tee, with views of Little Harbor and Wentworth Marina. A Sperry sailcloth tent (May to October) holds up to 250 guests. — [Wedding-Spot / directory listing via search excerpt](https://ws-render-core-weddingspot-prod.us-west-2.lb.cvent.com/venue/18250/wentworth-by-the-sea)
- Zola lists it in New Castle, starting at $15,000 for off-peak dates and $28,000 for peak dates. — [Zola listing](https://zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-venues/wentworth-by-the-sea-hotel-spa) (directory; peak months not defined)
- Implication: NOT a Clover venue. Mention it only as context, for example "Seacoast weddings from Wentworth to backyard tents".

**Wentworth-Coolidge Mansion State Historic Site (historic mansion, Portsmouth, on Little Harbor)**
- "Regretfully the site is no longer available for wedding receptions but can be rented for wedding ceremonies and small gatherings of 50 or fewer." Maximum capacity is 50. — [NH State Parks ceremony info sheet, July 2022](https://www.nhstateparks.org/getmedia/4328ef28-7ffb-42af-8e55-57ff3e14d5b4/ceremony-information-wentworth-coolige-historic-site-7-2022.pdf)
- "Alcohol allowed is beer and wine only. Beer may not be served in glass bottles." "Hiring of a police officer for the event is required if alcohol is served." — [same sheet](https://www.nhstateparks.org/getmedia/4328ef28-7ffb-42af-8e55-57ff3e14d5b4/ceremony-information-wentworth-coolige-historic-site-7-2022.pdf)
- Acoustic music only, no DJs. The renter handles cleanup. Portable toilets are required. Rental companies must be approved before the contract is signed. — [same sheet](https://www.nhstateparks.org/getmedia/4328ef28-7ffb-42af-8e55-57ff3e14d5b4/ceremony-information-wentworth-coolige-historic-site-7-2022.pdf)
- Implication: it could fit a small rehearsal dinner or intimate gathering with a beer-and-wine-only bar, but no cocktails. The sheet is dated 2022 and should be re-verified.

**Seacoast Science Center (oceanfront tent inside Odiorne Point State Park, 570 Ocean Blvd, Rye)**
- "All alcohol must be served by a caterer or insured bartending service with a valid NH liquor license." The bar service must have SSC's signed Alcohol and Intoxication Policy and service certifications on file. — [SSC Wedding Details (2026)](https://www.seacoastsciencecenter.org/visit/host-an-event/weddings/details/)
- A police detail is required if alcohol is served: "$95/hour, with a 4 hour minimum," subject to change per the Rye Police Department. — [SSC Wedding Details](https://www.seacoastsciencecenter.org/visit/host-an-event/weddings/details/)
- The Saturday tent rate is $9,750 plus fees, which includes the NH State Parks fee of $4/person. The tent is a 40'x90' "Bayview" white frame tent with full clear siding and electricity. Sperry Tents Seacoast is the exclusive tent and equipment vendor. Events are limited to 6 hours and must end by 11:00 pm (Rye noise ordinance). A professional coordinator is required. The center is booking 2026 and 2027. — [SSC Wedding Details](https://www.seacoastsciencecenter.org/visit/host-an-event/weddings/details/)
- The page links to a weather forecast and tide chart and carries a general notice about closing early for high winds. — [SSC Wedding Details](https://www.seacoastsciencecenter.org/visit/host-an-event/weddings/details/)
- Zola lists it as raw space with no in-house catering, up to 150 guests. — [Zola listing](https://zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-venues/seacoast-science-center)

**Strawbery Banke Museum (historic museum, Portsmouth, Puddle Dock neighborhood)**
- "Due to staffing transitions in the site rental program, Strawbery Banke is not currently booking rentals." The page also says the museum is no longer able to host wedding ceremonies and receptions. Contact: Alex Cave, Museum Events Manager. — [Strawbery Banke private events](https://strawberybanke.org/private-events)
- Implication: do NOT list Strawbery Banke as a venue Clover serves. It is fine to mention it as a landmark.

**Prescott Park (city waterfront park, Portsmouth)**
- "Alcoholic beverages are prohibited at all times in Prescott Park." A reservation approved by the Recreation Department is required for wedding ceremonies. Ceremonies of more than 75 people need special approval. Reservations do not make park space private. — [City of Portsmouth, Prescott Park wedding policies](https://www.cityofportsmouth.com/prescottpark/weddings-policies-and-procedures)
- The ban traces to the charitable trust of the Prescott sisters, who donated the land. Police have said they will enforce it with zero tolerance. — [WGME](https://wgme.com/news/local/portsmouth-steps-up-effort-to-stop-alcohol-use-in-park)
- Implication: a ceremony can happen at Prescott Park, but the bar must be at a separate reception site.

**Exeter Inn (1930s boutique hotel, 90 Front St, Exeter)**
- Wedding spaces include the Daniel Webster Ballroom, a courtyard with a gazebo and a sailcloth tent option, the Great Hall, and Epoch Restaurant & Bar for rehearsal dinners and showers. Capacity is about 100. — [Exeter Inn weddings](https://theexeterinn.com/events/weddings)
- Rental fee figures conflict: $2,500 for 12 hours per one listing, and $1,000 to $2,700 plus a 20% service charge per another. — [Wedding-Spot](https://www.wedding-spot.com/venue/12341/exeter-inn/); [Here Comes the Guide](https://www.herecomestheguide.com/wedding-venues/new-hampshire/the-exeter-inn)
- Its bar policy was not found. Because it has an in-house restaurant and bar, it most likely uses in-house bar service only (inference, unverified).

**Abenaqui Country Club (private club, 731 Central Rd, Rye Beach)**
- Member-owned club founded in 1903. Its function room holds up to 220 with an outdoor deck, and there is a ceremony space overlooking the golf course. It is wooded, not directly oceanfront. — [Wedding-Spot listing](https://wedding-spot.com/venue/13277/abenaqui-country-club)
- Its bar policy was not found. As a private club, it likely runs in-house bar service (inference, unverified).

**Saltonstall Farm (barn, Stratham)**
- Barn renovated in 2020 with a live-edge bar top. Up to 200 guests, from $9,000. Listed as raw space with no all-inclusive dining. — [Zola listing](https://zola.com/wedding-vendors/wedding-venues/saltonstall-farm); [WeddingWire](https://weddingwire.com/biz/saltonstall-farm/06c2348e188fe380.html)
- Its outside-bartender policy is NOT published. Raw-space venues usually bring in outside vendors, but this needs to be confirmed.

**Other venues found (policies not verified)**
- 1790 House (Newfields), outdoor, up to 140, from $3,750. Listed as all-inclusive with its own catering, so it is unlikely to allow outside bar service. — [Zola Exeter venues](https://zola.com/wedding-vendors/search/exeter-nh--wedding-venues)
- Churchill Barn Weddings and Events, The Barn at Union Lake Orchard, and Hyla Brook Estate are named by Zola as popular venues near Stratham and Rye. - Rivermill at Dover Landing (loft/mill-style event space, Dover) and The Hellenic Center (Dover, on-site catering). — [Wedding-Spot Rivermill](https://wedding-spot.com/venue/14039/rivermill-at-dover-landing); [Wedding-Spot Dover](https://www.wedding-spot.com/wedding-venues/new-hampshire/dover/)

### Inferences
- Clover's strongest fit is with private property (backyards, beach houses, family estates, tented farm or field weddings) and raw-space venues. Hotels, clubs, and inns on the Seacoast tend to keep the bar in-house.
- "Police detail required when alcohol is served" is a recurring local pattern (Wentworth by the Sea, Wentworth-Coolidge, SSC). Copy could note that Clover helps clients plan for venue requirements like police details and certificates of insurance, without claiming specific rates.
- The SSC rule ("valid NH liquor license") suggests some Seacoast venues will not accept an unlicensed BYOB bartender. Before Clover names any venue on the page, it should confirm what credentials it holds (insurance, alcohol-server certification, any license).

### Gaps
- Cocheco Mills (Dover): found nothing confirming it as a wedding venue.
- No published bar policies for Exeter Inn, Abenaqui, Saltonstall Farm, Portsmouth Atheneum, Moffatt-Ladd House, or Dover mill spaces.
- Hampton: no oceanfront Hampton wedding venues were verified. The Tuck Museum rental was not researched.
- The NH State Parks Seacoast Weddings & Events brochure exists ([PDF](https://www.nhstateparks.org/NHStateParks/media/NHStateParks/PDFs/Seacoast-Weddings-Events-Brochure.pdf)) but timed out on fetch. It probably lists Odiorne Point, Wallis Sands, Hampton Beach, and other state-park event options with their rules. Worth opening manually.
- Exact NH law on BYOB private events with hired bartenders was not confirmed. Secondary sources say NH has no statewide bartender license ([Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/bartending-license-in-new-hampshire)). A Dartmouth policy says events with no alcohol sale fall under "private events" per RSA 178 and need no state license ([Dartmouth guidelines](https://www.dartmouth.edu/cse/policies/alcohol_guidelines_11_24_21.pdf)). That is a university policy, not law. Check with the NH Liquor Commission.

## 2. Common event types on the Seacoast

### Takeaway
The evidence supports oceanfront tented weddings, small ceremonies with rehearsal dinners at historic sites, and a large corporate base at Pease. No sourced data turned up on how common backyard or beach-house parties are, though they fit Clover's model.

### Cited Findings
- Tented oceanfront weddings at Odiorne Point are an established format. One example is a 2018 tented wedding at Odiorne Point State Park by Exeter Events & Tents with the Seacoast Science Center. — [InTents magazine](https://intentsmag.com/2018/08/01/wedding-on-the-oceans-edge/)
- Wentworth-Coolidge Mansion now limits use to ceremonies and "small gatherings" of 50 or fewer, such as rehearsal dinners. — [NH State Parks sheet](https://www.nhstateparks.org/getmedia/4328ef28-7ffb-42af-8e55-57ff3e14d5b4/ceremony-information-wentworth-coolige-historic-site-7-2022.pdf)
- Exeter Inn markets Epoch Restaurant & Bar for rehearsal dinners and showers. — [Exeter Inn weddings](https://theexeterinn.com/events/weddings)
- Corporate base: Pease International Tradeport reports 13,212 direct jobs (2025 economic impact study, presented July 2026). — [Portsmouth EDC presentation, July 2026](https://files.portsmouthnh.gov/agendas/2026/Economic%20Development%20Commission/Portsmouth%20EDC%207-26%20Presentation.pdf). Earlier counts were over 250 companies and 10,500+ jobs (2020). — [EDC minutes 8/7/20](https://files.portsmouthnh.gov/agendas/2020/misc/EDC_MINUTES_8-7-20.pdf)

### Inferences
- Copy angles: "rehearsal dinner in Portsmouth," "tented wedding by the ocean," "company party for a Pease team," "holiday party." Each is plausible and fits these findings, but only the first two are directly evidenced.

### Gaps

## 3. Logistics for a mobile bar

### Takeaway
Alcohol is banned on NH state seacoast beaches and parks unless a special use permit or group reservation applies. Hampton also has a town open-container bylaw covering public beaches. Prescott Park bans alcohol entirely. So "bar on the beach" is generally not possible on public sand. The realistic setups are private property and permitted venues. Downtown Portsmouth has 30-minute loading zones and large garages.

### Cited Findings
- NH Admin Rule Res 7303.09(b) bans possession or consumption of alcohol at seacoast state parks, including Hampton Beach State Park, Wallis Sands State Park, Odiorne Point State Park, and Rye Harbor State Park, unless a special use permit or group reservation under Res 7400 allows it. — [Justia, Res 7303.09](https://regulations.justia.com/states/new-hampshire/res/chapter-res-7300/part-res-7303/section-res-7303-09) (current through Dec 2024 per the page; verify on the official NH site)
- The general rule Res 7301.25 allows alcohol on most state properties but carves out the seacoast beaches and some historic sites. — [Justia, Res 7301.25](https://regulations.justia.com/states/new-hampshire/res/chapter-res-7300/part-res-7301/section-res-7301-25)
- NH State Beach Patrol lists alcohol as not allowed on the beach. — [NH State Parks beach rules](https://www.nhstateparks.org/nh-state-beach-patrol/beach-rules-nh-state-beach-patrol)
- Enforcement: more than 9,000 alcohol violations were reported in one prior year across Hampton Beach, Jenness, and Wallis Sands (2024 article). — [AOL/Seacoastonline syndication](https://www.aol.com/hampton-beach-lifeguards-police-crack-203632542.html)
- Hampton's town open-container bylaw dates to 1981 and covers public streets, sidewalks, parking lots, the boardwalk, and public beaches. This comes from a 2004 article, so verify the current text. — [Lane Memorial Library history](https://history.lanememoriallibrary.org/?p=1650)
- Most NH towns ban public drinking, including at town beaches and parks. State-park beaches follow state rules. — [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/news/should-nh-allow-alcohol-consumption-public-beaches)
- Prescott Park bans alcohol at all times. — [City of Portsmouth](https://www.cityofportsmouth.com/prescottpark/weddings-policies-and-procedures)
- Portsmouth loading zones: only vehicles actively loading or unloading may park there, for a maximum of 30 minutes. A permit from the Parking Clerk does not guarantee a space. — [Portsmouth loading zone rules](https://www.portsmouthnh.gov/publicworks/parkportsmouth/loading-zone-permit-rules-regulations)
- Hanover Garage (34 Hanover St, 900 spaces, one block from Market Square) and Foundry Place Garage (100 Foundry Place, 24/7). Rates were $2/hr (Hanover, $40 max) and $1/hr (Foundry, $20 max); rates may have changed. — [Portsmouth City Garages](https://www.portsmouthnh.gov/node/727284)
- I-95 Hampton toll: $2.00 cash, about $1.40 with NH E-ZPass (2025, per a third-party guide). — [TollGuru](https://tollguru.mapup.ai/new-hampshire-toll-turnpike). A March 2026 page refers to an increase to $2.50, which is unverified and from a low-quality source. — [source](https://lam2.mzbusiness.com/how-much-are-the-nh-tolls.html). Verify with NHDOT before citing.
- Wind and weather: SSC links to a tide chart and has closed early for high winds. Its tent has full clear siding. — [SSC Wedding Details](https://www.seacoastsciencecenter.org/visit/host-an-event/weddings/details/)
- Rye's noise ordinance limits SSC events to an 11:00 pm end. — [SSC Wedding Details](https://www.seacoastsciencecenter.org/visit/host-an-event/weddings/details/)
- Seasonality: NH peak wedding season runs May through October ([NH cost guide, low-authority](https://new.risingsunartscentre.org/news/average-wedding-cost-in-new-hampshire-a-comprehensive-guide-2026.html)). Late spring through early fall is the best window for outdoor elements, and many venues close in late fall and winter ([WeddingWire NH guide](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-ideas/new-hampshire-wedding-guide)). Wentworth's tent runs May to October ([listing](https://ws-render-core-weddingspot-prod.us-west-2.lb.cvent.com/venue/18250/wentworth-by-the-sea)).

### Inferences
- Copy should not promise "beach bars." A safer phrasing is "beach house," "oceanfront backyard," or "tented by the water." Copy could add a plain note such as "public beaches and state parks on the Seacoast don't allow alcohol without a permit, so we'll help you plan around the venue rules."
- For downtown Portsmouth events, plan for 30-minute loading windows and garage parking.

### Gaps
- Route 1A summer traffic data and Rye or Portsmouth town permit processes for private events in town parks were not found.
- Rye town beach (Jenness) alcohol rule text was not retrieved. A Rye beach fire permit page exists ([Rye](https://www.ryenh.gov/895/Beach-Fire-Permit-Information)) but was not reviewed.
- Res 7400 special use permit terms were not reviewed.

## 5. Driving distances

### Takeaway
Portsmouth is about 44 to 45 road miles (about 46 minutes) from Manchester and about 60 miles from Concord. Dover is about 12 miles from Portsmouth.

### Cited Findings
- Portsmouth to Manchester is about 44 miles, about 46 minutes. — [mapsof.net](https://www.mapsof.net/distance/portsmouth-nh-to-manchester-nh); 45 miles per [DriveBestWay chart](https://www.drivebestway.com/mileage-chart-with-distances-between-cities/us-new-hampshire/)
- Portsmouth to Concord is about 60 miles. Dover is about 12 miles from Portsmouth, 39 from Manchester, and 39 from Concord. — [DriveBestWay chart](https://www.drivebestway.com/mileage-chart-with-distances-between-cities/us-new-hampshire/)

### Gaps
- Distances for Exeter, Hampton, Rye, and Durham were not sourced. Get them from Google Maps when writing.
